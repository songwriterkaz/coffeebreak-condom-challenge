Learn to use a condom the right way

This is a practice game, like the condom demonstration you might see in a school or clinic. You use a training model (or a banana) and put the steps in the right order.

Condoms help prevent STIs (sexually transmissible infections) and unplanned pregnancy when used correctly every time.

Steps are based on NSW Health Play Safe guidance.

Myths and facts

Consent comes first

  • Consent means everyone freely and clearly agrees. It's an enthusiastic yes, not silence or maybe.
  • Anyone can change their mind at any time, even partway through. Stop and check in.
  • Someone who is asleep, very drunk or high, or being pressured can't give consent.
  • In NSW the age of consent is 16.
  • You never have to do anything you don't want to do. Saying no is always okay.

If something has happened that didn't feel okay, you can talk to Kids Helpline any time on 1800 55 1800.
